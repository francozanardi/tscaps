Your captions live in the **Transcript** tab of Captions mode. Each card is one caption (the editor calls it a scene): the words on screen together.

## Fix a word
Click into a caption's card in **Transcript** and type, like in any text box. The word keeps its timing. For one word at a time, use the advanced view (the button with the tooltip **Switch to advanced mode (edit each word)**, desktop only), click the word and change **Word text**.

## Split a caption in two
Put the cursor where the new caption should start and press Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac). Pressing Enter alone starts a new line inside the same caption.

## Join two captions
Open the caption's **Scene options** (the ... button on its card) and choose **Join with previous scene** or **Join with next scene**. You can also press Backspace at the very start of a caption's text, or Delete at its very end.

## Change when a caption shows
Open **Scene options** and choose **Edit timing**. In **Scene timing**, type a **Start** and **End** or drag the slider. **Redistribute** spreads the word times across the caption by speaking pace. In the advanced view, click a word to set its own **Start** and **End**. You can also drag words and captions in **Timeline** mode; its **Edit by** menu switches between words and captions.

## Hide a caption without deleting it
In **Scene options**, choose **Hide from video**. The words stay in the transcript but do not show in the video. Click the crossed-out eye on the card, or choose **Show in video**, to bring it back. Very short captions are hidden on their own while **Hide brief scenes** is on in **Transcript actions** (the wand button).

## Delete words or captions
Select the words in a caption's text and delete them, or in the advanced view click a word and choose **Delete word**. **Delete scene** in **Scene options** removes a whole caption. For many at once, open **Transcript actions**, then **Select multiple**, pick **Words**, click the words, then **Actions** and **Delete words**.

## Find a word
Click the search button above the transcript and type in **Find in scenes…**.
