Templates draw some words differently, such as in an accent colour or bigger, based on tags the words carry. Emojis sit on words and show with their caption.

## Make a word stand out (tags)
1. In **Transcript**, switch to the advanced view (desktop only) and click the word.
2. Choose **Edit tags** and switch tags on or off in **Word tags**. Hover a tag to read what it means.

Only the tags the current template draws are listed. A template with key phrases, such as Aris, lists them last, marked with the templates icon: switch it on for words to put them in a key phrase, or off to take them out. That tag is the template's own, so applying another template sets it again. If **Edit tags** is greyed out, this template does not use tags: try another template, or give the word its own style with **Edit style**. Some tags are added on their own when your captions are made. For many words at once: **Transcript actions**, **Select multiple**, **Words**, then **Actions** and **Edit tags**.

## Add, change or remove an emoji
On a caption's card in **Transcript**, click **Add emoji** (the smiley) and choose one in **Pick emoji**. It attaches to the caption's last word. Click an emoji on a card to **Change emoji**, **Edit style** or **Remove emoji**.

## Where emojis sit and how big
In the **Effects** tab, under **Emojis**: **Placement** puts them **Below**, **Above** or **Around** the caption, or **Inline** beside their word. **Size** and **Gap** set their size and distance.

## Lowercase and punctuation
In the **Effects** tab:
- **Smart lowercase**: every word in lowercase, except names and "I".
- **Remove punctuation**: drops commas, periods, colons and ellipses at the end of words; question and exclamation marks stay.
- **Smart punctuation**: typographic quotes, apostrophes, dashes and ellipses.
- **Carry quotes**: repeats the quote marks on each caption of a quote that spans several.
- **Gap-free**: keeps each caption on screen until the next one starts.

They apply to the selected caption style (sheet) and change only how captions look; your transcript text is kept. For all capitals, use **Case** in **Typography**.
