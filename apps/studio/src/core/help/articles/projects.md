A project keeps your video and everything you did to it, so you can come back later. This article is about projects stored in this browser on this device.

## Save your work
Your project is saved once the transcription finishes. After that, click **Save** in the editor's top bar to keep your changes, notes you marked on the video included. The label next to it says **Unsaved changes**, **Saved** or **Save failed**. If you try to leave with unsaved changes, the browser asks first.

## Open a project
Click **Back to dashboard** (the arrow at the top left of the editor) to reach your projects page (**Local projects** for projects stored in this browser), then click a project's card. **New project** starts one from a new video. A card marked **Unfinished** stopped before its captions were made: opening it brings back the video so you can start again.

## Rename a project
In the editor, click the project's name in the top bar (**Project name**), type the new name, and press Enter. Escape keeps the old name.

## Delete a project
On the projects page, open the card's menu (**Project actions**), choose **Delete**, and confirm. This cannot be undone.

## Back up a project or move it to another device
In the card's menu, **Export** downloads a `.tscaps` file with your editing work. It does not include the video. On the other browser or device, click **Import .tscaps** on the projects page, then pick the same video file when asked.

## Where projects are kept
Projects live in this browser's storage on this device. They are not shared with other browsers or devices, and clearing the browser's site data deletes them, so export a `.tscaps` file of anything you want to keep.
