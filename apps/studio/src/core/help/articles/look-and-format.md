Both live in the **Video** mode: **Color** grades the whole picture, and **Ratio** sets the shape the video is exported in.

## Give the video a colour look
1. In the **Video** mode, open the **Color** tab.
2. Under **Look**, pick one (such as Warm, Vivid, Moody or Black & white). Each is shown on a frame of your video.
3. Fine-tune under **Adjust**: **Exposure**, **Contrast**, **Saturation**, **Temperature**, **Tint** and **Vignette**.

The grade covers the video, b-roll and pictures, never the captions. Pick **None** to remove it.

## Change the frame format (vertical, square, wide)
1. In the **Video** mode, open the **Ratio** tab.
2. Pick **Original**, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9.
3. Under **Video**, choose **Size**: **Fill** crops the video to fill the frame; **Fit** shows all of it with a **Background** that is **Blurred** or **Black**.
4. With **Fill**, drag the box on the frame to choose which part of the video shows.

That box holds one crop for the whole video.

Captions and shot layers are laid out on the new shape.
