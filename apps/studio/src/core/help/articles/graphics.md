A graphic is a small animated design drawn in a box over a shot, below the captions: a hook title, a name and role, a key number or phrase, a quote, a list item, a place name, a call to follow, a progress bar. You start from a preset and change its text, or edit its code.

## Add a graphic to a shot
1. In the **Video** mode, open the **Shots** tab.
2. Click **Add** at the bottom of the shot card and choose **Graphic**.
3. The graphic opens beside the preview on its **Presets** tab. Click a preset to use it, or type in **Search presets**.

Picking another preset replaces the graphic.

## Change the text and colours
Under the presets, the **Content** section lists what the preset lets you fill in, such as its text, colours, a picture, or an emoji (click **Choose emoji**). Changes show in the preview a moment after you stop typing. **Hide captions on this shot** keeps the captions off while the graphic shows.

## Place and size the box
Click the box on the preview to pick it, then drag it to move it and drag a corner to resize it. The **Layout** tab sets the same things with controls. **Motion** sets how the whole box enters and leaves, on top of the graphic's own animation, and **Sound** puts sound effects on its moments.

## Edit, swap or remove a graphic
Click the graphic's pill on its shot card: **Edit** opens it, where **Presets** swaps it for another; **Duration** keeps it on screen over the shots that follow (pick the last shot it shows on); **Remove** takes it off.

## Edit its code
The **Code** tab holds the graphic's **HTML**, **CSS** and **JS**, and **Box** for the box's own CSS. **Console** shows what the code logged or any error it hit. A graphic whose code you changed shows as **Custom**. A graphic has no access to the internet.

## Save your own preset
On the **Presets** tab, click **Save as a new preset** and name it. Your presets are kept in this browser and listed first; you can rename or delete them from their cards.
