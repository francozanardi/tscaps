Cuts take stretches out of the video. You make them in the **Timeline** mode, where the words sit on rows at the times they are spoken. A cut is never final: the timeline keeps showing what was cut so you can bring it back.

## Remove all the silences at once
1. Open the **Timeline** mode in the editor's sidebar.
2. Click the scissors button (**Cuts**) in the timeline's toolbar.
3. Choose **Remove silences**.
4. Pick how tight the result should be: **Natural**, **Fast** or **Faster**. The button below says how many silences it will remove.
5. Click **Remove N silences**. A notice says how much video was removed and how long the video now runs.

The menu stays open, so you can try another setting and remove more.

## Cut one part of the video
1. In the **Timeline** mode, press and drag across the rows over the part you want gone. The selection loops while you look at it.
2. Click **Cut** in the small bar that appears next to it, or press Delete or Backspace. **Cancel** drops the selection.

In the **Scenes** view you can also open a scene's menu and choose **Cut this scene**. To cut one pause, in the **Words** view click the silence between two words: it gets selected, then click **Cut**.

## Bring back something you cut
- One cut: click the shaded cut on the timeline and choose **Restore this cut**.
- Every cut: open **Cuts** (the scissors) and choose **Restore all cuts**.
- Or use **Undo** in the top bar.

## Show the waveform
The waveform is off until you turn it on. Click the eye button (**View**) in the timeline's toolbar and turn on **Waveform**: a strip under each row shows where the sound is, which helps you see pauses. In the same menu, **Edit by** switches between **Words** and **Scenes**.
