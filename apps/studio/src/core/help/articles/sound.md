Sounds live in the **Video** mode's **Audio** tab: music under the whole video, the level of the video's own sound, and every sound effect you placed. Everything plays in the preview and is mixed into the export.

## Add background music
1. In the **Video** mode, open the **Audio** tab.
2. Under **Music**, click **Add music**.
3. Upload an audio file of your own, or pick one you added before, and click **Use**.

Music runs under the whole video. Set its **Volume**, **Fade in** and **Fade out**, and **Loop**. Turn on **Lower under speech** to make it quieter while someone talks, and **Lower by** to say how much. **Music options** (the dots) lets you **Change music** or remove it. An audio file in the **Media** tab can also be made the music from its menu.

## Add a sound effect on a word
Click a word (in the captions' transcript, or in a shot card's text) and choose **Add sound at this word…**. Pick a sound under **Built-in**, or upload one under **Your files**. The sound follows the word if you edit the timing.

## Add a sound effect on a shot, a layer or a transition
- A shot: click **Add** on its card and choose **Sound**, then **Add sound** at its start or end.
- A b-roll, picture or graphic: open it and use its **Sound** tab, at its entrance or exit.
- A transition or a camera move: its panel has its own **Add sound**.

Every effect you placed is listed under **Sound effects** in the **Audio** tab, with its **Volume** and a nudge earlier or later.

Some templates bring sounds of their own, the way graphics and transitions do: Aris taps on each word of its key phrases as it appears. Applying the template places them as sound effects like any other, listed in the **Audio** tab, where you can change or remove each one; switching to another template takes them away.


## Change the video's own volume
In the **Audio** tab, **Video sound** has a **Volume** slider; at zero it shows **Muted**. A single shot's video sound can be set on that shot's **Sound** tab.
