A template is a ready-made caption design: font, colours and animation. You pick one, then adjust it in the other tabs of Captions mode.

## Pick a template
1. Open the **Templates** tab.
2. Browse the families, or type in **Search templates**. **View all** opens a whole family.
3. Click a card to put it on your captions.

Click the star (**Add to favorites**) on a card to keep it under **Favorites**.

## Adjust the look
- **Typography**: **Font**, **Font size**, weight, **Case** (uppercase or lowercase), spacing.
- **Style**: the template's own settings, such as colours. Some templates offer a **Preset** with ready variations.
- **Position**: where the captions sit in the frame. You can also drag a caption in the preview.
- **Motion**: how captions, words and emojis come in and leave.
- **Layout**: how the words are cut into captions and each caption into lines. A template with key phrases, such as Aris, shows a tab for its small captions and one for its key phrases, each with its own settings.

Each tab has a **Reset to template default** button that undoes your changes in that tab.

## Write your own CSS
The **Code** tab holds the template's source for your captions: **CSS** and **SVG Filters**. Edit it for looks the other tabs do not offer. A setting in the other tabs that your CSS no longer uses is dimmed.

## Save a look as your own template
In the **Templates** tab, click **Save current style as a new template**, type a **Template name** and click **Save**. It appears under **Saved**, ready for your next video.

## Style one word or one caption differently
Click the word or caption in the preview. A panel opens just for it, with its own **Style**, **Motion**, **Effects** and **Code** tabs; what you set there applies on top of the template. From the transcript, click a word in the advanced view and choose **Open in inspector** or **Edit style**. **Reset this element** in the panel's header removes its own changes.

To give a whole group of captions another template (a hook, a second speaker), see the caption styles (sheets) article.
